Privacy Policy

Policy prepared: 21 October 2026. App: Borderless Local. Contact: mahbubhasanoly@gmail.com.

Using the guide

Borderless Local provides travel guides and city information. The Android app bundles country and city data on your device. Browsing that bundled directory does not require an account. This version has no advertising SDK or developer-operated analytics service.

Optional location

Location is requested only when you choose a location or directions feature. Android may ask for approximate location permission. The guide compares coordinates with guide city centres on the device; it does not send those coordinates to the developer's rules server. If you request directions, the website opens Google Maps with coordinates and a destination. Google then handles that request under its own privacy policy. You may deny location permission and continue choosing cities manually. The app does not request background location.

Online content and third parties

Online pictures, article discovery and source links can contact Wikipedia/Wikimedia. Optional translation sends public interface or guide text and the selected language to MyMemory. Maps and other external links open the selected provider. These requests disclose information needed for the request, such as the city, place, search text, language or coordinates, and can expose your IP address and technical request information to that provider. Provider availability and retention are controlled by those providers.

Optional rules server

If you configure a rules server, the app sends requests for available cities and selected city rules, including city identifiers and language. That server receives network request information, including your IP address. Do not enter passwords or secret tokens in a server URL. A server you choose is operated under its own privacy and retention practices. Android release builds require HTTPS connections.

Local storage and deletion

The app stores the configured server address locally and may cache translations and web resources. You can clear the server address in settings. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local settings and caches. No in-app account is created, so there is no app account to delete. Third-party request records are subject to each provider's retention and deletion process.

Contact and updates

If you email support, your address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, passport details or other unnecessary sensitive information. Ask privacy or deletion questions at mahbubhasanoly@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when app data practices change.
