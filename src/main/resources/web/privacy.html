Privacy Policy

Updated: 5 November 2026. App: Borderless Local Assistant (website, Android and Windows). Contact: mahbubhasanoly@gmail.com.

Using the guide

Borderless Local provides travel guides and city information. The native Android and Windows apps bundle country and city data on your device. Browsing that bundled directory does not require an account. This version has no advertising SDK or developer-operated analytics service.

Optional location

Location is requested only when you choose a location or directions feature. Android may offer approximate or precise location permission; Windows uses its foreground location permission and service settings. The guide compares coordinates with guide city centres on the device; it does not send those coordinates to the developer's rules server. If you request directions, the website opens Google Maps with coordinates and a destination. Google then handles that request under its own privacy policy. You may deny location permission and continue choosing cities manually. The app does not request background location.

Online content and third parties

Online pictures, article discovery and source links can contact Wikipedia/Wikimedia. Optional translation sends public interface or guide text and the selected language to MyMemory. Maps and other external links open the selected provider. These requests disclose information needed for the request, such as the city, place, search text, language or coordinates, and can expose your IP address and technical request information to that provider. Provider availability and retention are controlled by those providers.

Optional rules server

The native app uses the default HTTPS rules service at borderless-local-assistant.onrender.com, hosted on Render, unless its address is changed or cleared in Settings. If you use a rules server, the app sends requests for available cities and selected city rules, including city identifiers and language. That server receives network request information, including your IP address. Do not enter passwords or secret tokens in a server URL. A server you choose is operated under its own privacy and retention practices. Android release builds require HTTPS connections.

Local storage and deletion

The native app stores its server address, interface language, Saved and Recent guide records and checklist selections locally (Android preferences or Windows application preferences). The website may cache translations and web resources in browser storage. You can remove Saved guides in the app and clear the server address in Settings; this does not disable online photos or article discovery. Android Clear storage, Windows app Reset where available, or clearing browser site data removes local settings for that installation. Uninstall and restoration behavior depends on the operating system and backup settings; OS backups may retain app data. No in-app account is created, so there is no app account to delete. Third-party request records are subject to each provider's retention and deletion process.

Contact and updates

If you email support, your address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, passport details or other unnecessary sensitive information. Ask privacy or deletion questions at mahbubhasanoly@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when app data practices change.
